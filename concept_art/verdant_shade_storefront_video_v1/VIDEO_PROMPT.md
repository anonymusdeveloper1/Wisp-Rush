# Shade — storefront idle video (image-to-video)

The looping idle that plays behind Shade's Shop card and as the Home hero, replacing the 12-frame
`storefront_idle` sprite loop. Generated by an image-to-video model from `idle_hover_00`, the
front-facing pose from the approved sprite pack. The game only ever shows this in a menu, where the
character is large and still, so this clip is the character's sales pitch.

## Files in this folder

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `shade_reference_magenta.png` | **Upload this one.** 1024 × 1024, Shade centred on flat `#FF00FF` |
| `shade_reference_transparent.png` | The same framing with a transparent background, for tools that insist on one — expect them to invent a background |
| `source/` | Put the raw generated MP4s here (create it). Never edit them; the game's copy is derived |

## Why the background is magenta, not transparent

Image-to-video models only export opaque video: no Kling, Runway, Veo, Luma or Hailuo output has an
alpha channel. So the model paints Shade on one flat colour, and the pipeline removes that colour
afterwards (see *After generation*). The colour has to be one that appears nowhere on the character:

- **not green** — the usual green screen would eat Shade's flames, eyes, gem and leaf edges;
- **not black** — the hood and leaves are near-black;
- **not white** — the mask face is off-white;
- **magenta** sits directly opposite green on the colour wheel, so it separates cleanly.

Most models copy the background of the image they are given, which is why the reference is already
on magenta. That does more than any sentence in the prompt.

## Tool settings

| Setting | Value | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Start frame | `shade_reference_magenta.png` | |
| End frame | **the same image**, if the tool has an end-frame / last-frame option | Forces the clip to finish where it began, which is what makes it loop |
| Aspect ratio | **1:1** | Fits the card and the Home hero |
| Duration | **5 s** (4 s is fine) | One slow breath cycle; longer clips drift further from the reference |
| Resolution | highest available, at least 720p | The game plays it back at ≤ 720p |
| Frame rate | 24 or 30 fps | Either is fine; the pipeline resamples |
| Camera | **static / fixed / locked**, if the tool has a camera control | |
| Motion strength | **low** (or low–medium) | High values invent actions and move the camera |
| Prompt enhancer / "magic prompt" | **off** | Enhancers rewrite the prompt and tend to add camera moves and scenery |
| Audio | off | The game supplies its own sound |
| Variants | generate 3–4 and pick | Most takes fail one check below; that is normal |

## Prompt — full version

Use this where the tool accepts long prompts. It is kept under 2,500 characters, Kling's limit.

```
A seamless looping idle animation of the character in the reference image, for a video game shop
screen. Static locked-off camera, front view. The character stays centred and the same size in
every frame.

CHARACTER - keep it exactly as in the reference: a small hooded forest spirit. A pointed dark green
hood made of overlapping leaves. A smooth pale bone-white mask for a face, with two glowing green
almond-shaped eyes and no mouth. A small glowing green gem on the chest. A coat of layered dark
green leaves with bright green edges. Two curling green flame horns rising from the top of the
hood. A thin glowing green flame tail that curls below the body instead of legs. It has no arms,
no hands, no legs and no feet, and it must not grow any.

MOTION - subtle, slow and calm. This is a breathing idle, not an action:
- The whole body floats gently up and down by a very small amount, in one slow cycle over the full
  clip, and ends exactly where it started.
- The two flame horns flicker and sway softly like candle flames, their tips curling and uncurling.
- The leaf coat breathes: the leaves lift and settle slightly, and the outer leaf tips ripple.
- The flame tail sways slowly from side to side and curls, like smoke in still air.
- The chest gem pulses softly brighter and dimmer.
- The eyes glow steadily, with at most one slow, calm blink in the middle of the clip.
- The hood and mask keep facing the camera; the head may tilt a few degrees and back.

LOOP: every movement is cyclical and returns to the starting pose. The last frame matches the first
frame exactly, so the clip repeats with no visible jump.

BACKGROUND: a perfectly flat, solid, uniform magenta background (#FF00FF) in every pixel of every
frame. No gradient, no vignette, no texture, no floor, no ground shadow, no environment.

LIGHTING: keep the character's own lighting from the reference. The green glow comes from the
flames, the eyes and the chest gem. The magenta background does not light the character: no pink
or purple reflections, rim light or colour cast on it. The glow stays close around the flames and
does not spread a haze across the background.

FRAMING: the whole character stays fully inside the frame at all times, with empty background above
the flame tips and below the tail. Nothing touches or leaves the edges of the frame.

STYLE: the same painted 2D game-art style as the reference - crisp, sharp edges, the same detail
in every frame. No motion blur.
```

## Prompt — short version

For tools with a tight prompt limit (Runway, Luma). About 800 characters.

```
Seamless looping idle animation of this exact character, static locked camera, front view, centred,
same size every frame. Keep the design identical: leaf hood, white mask with two green eyes and no
mouth, green chest gem, dark leaf coat, two green flame horns, flame tail instead of legs; no arms,
hands or legs. Slow calm breathing: body floats up and down very slightly, flame horns flicker like
candles, leaves lift and settle, tail sways like smoke, chest gem pulses softly, one slow blink.
Every motion returns to the start pose; last frame matches the first. Flat solid magenta #FF00FF
background in every frame, no gradient, floor or shadow. No pink light on the character; glow stays
tight around the flames. Whole character always inside the frame. Crisp painted 2D game art, no
motion blur.
```

## Reject the take if

Watch every candidate looped at least three times before keeping it.

- [ ] **The background is not flat magenta everywhere** — any gradient, darker corner, floor or
      shadow under the character. This is the one that decides whether the pipeline can cut it out.
- [ ] **The character changed**: a mouth, hands, a different hood, extra flames, a new colour.
- [ ] **The camera moved**, or Shade drifted sideways, grew or shrank.
- [ ] **It jumps at the loop point** — the last frame does not lead into the first.
- [ ] **Anything touches a frame edge**, even for one frame (usually a flame tip).
- [ ] **There is a pink or purple cast** on the leaves, the mask or the glow.
- [ ] **The leaves boil or morph** from frame to frame instead of moving.

A take that passes everything except a small loop jump is still worth keeping: the pipeline can
blend a few frames across the seam.

## After generation (built 2026-09-21 — ADR-0016)

Run `GODOT_PATH=<Godot editor binary> python tools/art/extract_menu_video.py verdant_shade`; for a
new take, add it to `VIDEO_PACKS` there (source, loop frame, crossfade, label mask). It does:

1. Raw MP4 into `source/`.
2. Remove the magenta with a keyer plus magenta despill, cross-checked against an AI matte, so the
   soft flame edges keep their glow instead of a pink fringe.
3. Pack colour and alpha side by side in one frame, since Godot's only video codec (Ogg Theora) has
   no alpha, and encode at ≤ 720p / 30 fps with **Godot's own Movie Maker** — Windows ffmpeg writes
   broken Theora. The tool checks the frame count and rate of what it wrote.
4. A small shader recombines the two halves; only the focused Shop card and the Home hero play the
   video, the neighbouring cards show the still portrait, and Reduced Motion shows the portrait.
5. The project setting *Video Delay Compensation* stays at 0, or the loop stops being seamless.
