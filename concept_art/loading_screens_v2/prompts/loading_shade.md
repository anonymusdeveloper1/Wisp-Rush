# loading_shade

Built-in Codex image generator. Input references are listed in generation.json in the same order.

Use case: illustration-story. Create a single portrait 1080 x 2400 Wisp Rush loading illustration without text or interface.
Reference 1: the exact approved SHADE character identity. Reference 2: pixel-art world/rendering quality only; invent a different composition, do not copy its ledge or side lantern.
Scene: Shade hovers in a moonlit grove on a broken floating island. Ancient twisted roots grip the edges of a small ruined circular stone well; open root arches frame the far left and right. His green spirit tail curls above the well's opening, throwing green light across the old mossy stone. A few small leaves turn in the air beside him, and two little green spirit wisps hover in the side foliage. Beyond this island is a deep open night sky with sparse distant tree-covered stone fragments. Clear coherent depth, dark foreground roots, readable hero in the middle, distant silhouettes.
Copy Shade's exact small spectral proportions: smooth ivory mask with two long glowing green eyes, pointed dark-green hood and two curling lime-green flame horns, layered leaf-shaped dark-green cloak shoulders and luminous green chest jewel. He has NO human legs, no human arms carrying weapons, and ends in a bright curled green wisp tail. Keep the same mask and outfit; gentle midair lean with the leaf cloak caught by a breeze, calm watchful pose. Green horns and tail must retain their reference silhouettes. No replacement humanoid, no skull, no sword.
Composition for a loading background: Shade's horns begin at 43 percent of canvas height; his mask is around 49 percent; tail ends at 73 percent. Main silhouette large enough to read on a phone, centered slightly right above the well. Central upper 38 percent is subdued open night sky with low contrast and no bright forms so a separate logo can sit there. Bottom 18 percent is dark, quiet island underside/root silhouettes for a separate progress bar. Keep all important character edges safely inside the frame.
Polished 2D PIXEL ART only, matching the established game and the reference's character colors. Crisp visible pixel clusters, limited palette, stepped contours, discrete shade blocks; don't add heavy black character outlines. No painterly art, no 3D rendering, no smooth gradients, no blur, no anti-aliasing. Emerald/lime accents against charcoal, moss and muted slate teal. One whole staged scene, opaque full bleed, no captions, letters, logo, UI, borders, collage or watermark.
