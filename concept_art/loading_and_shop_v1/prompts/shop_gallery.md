Design a quiet suspended stone display gallery in the SAME world as the loading image. A simple half-ruined roofless stone hall opens into the void, with distant floating ruined arches seen through its rear opening. Two substantial weathered slate-stone columns stand at the side edges, grounded into the gallery floor. Each column carries a small warm amber lantern on a visibly attached curved iron bracket. These should suggest a character/cosmetic gallery, not a weapon shop: no weapons, money, treasure piles, merchants or unapproved characters. Two tiny cyan wisps hover close to the side architecture. Use only a little moss in the column cracks.

Keep the upper 16% quiet and dark for the existing Shop header and tabs. Keep the entire central 64% of image width between x=18% and x=82%, from y=18% to y=78%, as a dark calm uninterrupted view into the void. Style/medium: authentic polished 2D PIXEL ART matching reference 1. All world surfaces use crisp square pixel clusters on one grid, a limited palette, discrete flat color steps and sparse deliberate texture. No painterly rendering, 3D rendering, blur, gradients, photographic lighting, bloom, vector-smooth edges or anti-aliasing. Make depth through overlapping silhouettes and successively quieter solid colors. Void charcoal #111521, slate teal #263D42, worn ivory cloth and muted leather browns, amber #F3A847 limited to the two lanterns, cyan #62E8F2 and white #EAFDFF limited to the small wisps, moss #5E7D4C sparingly. The center stays darker than the edges. Create a memorable usable game backdrop, not an overdecorated fantasy illustration.
