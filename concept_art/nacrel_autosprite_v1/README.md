# Nacrel — AutoSprite first-frame review

Status: **concept art for owner approval.** No game assets or Godot files were changed.

## Design

Nacrel is a slim lantern wanderer based primarily on the first supplied reference: a tall pale angular hood, two long feather plumes, midnight face with small mint eyes, dark segmented armour with cyan seams, layered white-and-teal cape, and a straight black staff with a warm yellow lantern. The second reference informs only restrained cream ornament on the cloak. The staff stays in her right hand throughout.

Other name ideas: **Veilune**, **Paleglint**, **Auramoth**, **Lumenward**, **Mothmere**.

The right-wall pose was revised from the owner's attached raw image so the lower leg has a visible bent knee and both boots read as separate limbs. The optional top pose hangs head-down from an invisible ceiling, with the lantern staff still in her right hand.

## Conversion

For each image-model source, pixels with alpha below 3 were cleared to remove generated background haze. Small detached specks were removed. The visible art was reduced at the common scale using premultiplied-colour area sampling, quantized to at most 256 RGBA colours with soft alpha edges retained, centred on a transparent 256 × 256 canvas, and enlarged 4× using nearest neighbour. No outline, environment, wall, floor or effects were added.
