# Verdant Shade — playable-creature concept v1

Status: **owner review only; not integrated into the game**.

`Verdant Shade` is a working title for a new handless playable creature. The concept uses the
owner-provided Pinterest posts as mood and movement references while keeping the design original
and rendering it in the established Wisp Rush painterly style.

The current owner-directed shape is the softer hooded wraith introduced in `concept_board_v2.png`.
`concept_board_v3_blue_cyan.png` applies the newest color direction: a midnight-blue hood and
cloak, moonstone mask, and spectral cyan-blue flames. The earlier green and armored-seed boards
remain available for comparison.

## Core design

- No arms, hands, fingers, humanoid torso, legs or held weapon.
- A charcoal pointed hood and layered leaf-cloak form the floating silhouette.
- A pale moss-stone seed mask carries two large dark eye openings with green light inside.
- Twin acid-green ghost-flame horns provide strong motion and aim direction.
- Green light leaks from a protected core beneath the mask and through the cloak seams.
- Three spectral root-ribbons provide secondary motion and wall anchoring.
- The dash folds the complete hood, mask and cloak into its own cutting arrowhead.

## Gameplay readability

- **Idle:** root-filaments drift independently while the armor remains stable.
- **Aim/charge:** eyes and core brighten; filaments point toward the aim vector.
- **Dash attack:** the complete body compresses into a forward green crescent/leaf-blade.
- **Wall contact:** the body flattens toward the surface and three root-filaments form simple
  hooked anchors with no hands or fingers.
- **Hit:** armor plates recoil inward around the core.
- **Death/reform:** the shell breaks into dark leaf fragments and green motes, then rebuilds.
- **Storefront:** a calm three-quarter hover exposes the bright core and expressive eyes.
