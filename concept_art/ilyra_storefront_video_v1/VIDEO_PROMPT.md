# Ilyra — storefront idle video (image-to-video)

The looping idle that plays on Ilyra's Shop card and as the Home hero, the way Shade's does
(ADR-0016). Generated by an image-to-video model from `idle_hover`, her front-facing pose from the
approved sprite pack. The game shows it only in a menu, where she is large and still, so this clip
is the character's sales pitch.

Shade's brief is the template — `concept_art/verdant_shade_storefront_video_v1/VIDEO_PROMPT.md`.
What is different here is the key colour and her design, which is much harder for a video model.

## Why the background is green here, not magenta

The key colour has to be one the character never uses. Measured on her solid pixels — the share a
key would start eating:

| Key | Ilyra | Shade |
|---|---:|---:|
| magenta `#FF00FF` | 5.3 % — her lavender skin and violet fan tips | 0.0 % |
| green `#00FF00` | **0.0 %** | 32.4 % |
| blue `#0000FF` | 27.8 % — her fans | 0.0 % |

So Ilyra is keyed on green and Shade on magenta. Most models copy the background of the image they
are given, which is why the reference is already on green.

## Tool settings

| Setting | Value | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Start frame | `ilyra_reference_green.png` | |

| Aspect ratio | **1:1** | Fits the card and the Home hero |
| Duration | **5 s** | One slow breath; longer clips drift further from the reference |
| Resolution | highest available, at least 720p | |
| Camera | **static / fixed / locked** | |
| Motion strength | **low** | Matters more for her than for Shade: every extra degree of motion is a chance for an arm or a hand to break |
| Prompt enhancer / "magic prompt" | **off** | |
| Audio | off | |
| Negative prompt | none — the tool has no field for one; the prompt carries every constraint as a positive rule | |
| Variants | generate 4–6 and pick | Expect more rejects than Shade's: four arms and two fans are exactly where these models slip |

## Prompt (BytePlus Lumina / Seedance)

```
The fantasy character from the image floats gently in place, calm and graceful, like slow breathing. The two upper hands hold the open fans and sway them softly; the two lower hands drift in a slow, gentle wave. The long hair sways, the outfit's long fabric panels ripple, and the three small gems above the head bob and sparkle. One slow blink. Plain solid pure green background in every frame. Fixed camera, front view, centred, same size in every frame. Painted 2D game art, crisp detail. Seamless loop: the last frame matches the first.
```

94 words — Seedance's guidance is 60–100, with the first 20–30 carrying the most weight — in its
order: subject, action, environment, camera, style, constraints. In Lumina also use the
**first-and-last-frame mode** with this same image as both frames (that is what makes it loop) and
the **fixed-camera** option if the model offers it.

**Why this wording.** The first two versions were refused with *"The input text may contain sensitive
information"* (code 23003). Seedance reports text and image refusals separately, so that message
means the prompt text itself; BytePlus's advice is to remove anything that reads as violent,
political or adult. Two words sat in both refused prompts and in none that passed: **"arms"**
(readable as weapons) and **"crystals"** (drug slang). This version has neither — four arms are
"the two upper hands … the two lower hands", the crown is "three small gems" — and it never
describes her body, age or skin: the image carries the design. Lumina has no negative-prompt field,
so every constraint is stated positively.

Sources: BytePlus FAQ on sensitive-content rejections; Segmind's Seedance 2.0 error guide (the text
vs image messages); the Seedance 2.5 prompt guide mirrored at github.com/Anil-matcha/awesome-seedance-2.5-api-prompts
(the docs.byteplus.com pages render client-side and could not be read directly).

## Variant: ready stance (owner, 2026-09-21)

The owner wants her idle to read as an attack-ready pose — hands ready, legs, braids and the fabric
panels around her legs moving. Start from `ilyra_ready_reference_green.png`: the game's
`aim_charge.png` (her charge-up stance, the only uncut combat-ready painting) on green, 1024², with
`ilyra_ready_clean.png` as the clean transparent original. Green is still safe here: 0.0 % of her
solid pixels, the blue energy swirl included. Same settings as above — this image as both first and
last frame, fixed camera, 1:1, 5 s.

```
The fantasy character from the image holds a poised ready stance. The two lower hands gather the glowing blue energy, pulsing and swirling. The two upper hands hold the open fans high and steady, tilting them only slowly with each breath. The legs bend as the weight shifts side to side, feet wide. The braids swing and the pointed fabric panels around the legs flutter. The three gems above the head bob and sparkle. Plain solid pure green background in every frame. Fixed camera, front view, centred, same size. Crisp painted 2D game art. Seamless loop: the last frame matches the first.
```

Take 1 (`2026-09-21_20-39-06_Lumina.mp4`) kept the four arms, the stance and a flat background, but "flick them" made the upper hands snap the fans back and forth every ~0.6 s with speed-trail arcs, and those snaps doubled the loop-seam jump. The fan line now holds them high and steady and lets them tilt only slowly.

About 100 words. It avoids every violent word (*attack*, *fight*, *strike*, *combat*, *weapon*) as
well as *arms* and *crystals*: the pose is a "poised ready stance", which the image makes
unambiguous.

## Reject the take if

Watch every candidate looped at least three times, and **count her arms on a paused frame** at the
start, the middle and the end.

- [ ] **She does not have exactly four arms in every frame** — this is the likeliest failure.
- [ ] **A hand breaks**: extra or fused fingers, a hand melting into a fan handle.
- [ ] **A fan closes, drops, changes colour or swaps hands.**
- [ ] **The crown lands on her head, merges into her hair or loses a crystal.**
- [ ] **The background is not flat green everywhere** — any gradient, darker corner or shadow.
- [ ] **The camera moved**, or she drifted sideways, grew or shrank.
- [ ] **It jumps at the loop point.** (A small jump is fixable: the intake blends across the seam.)
- [ ] **Anything touches a frame edge**, even for one frame — usually a fan tip or a braid.
- [ ] **There is a green cast** on her skin, hair or the fans' edges.
